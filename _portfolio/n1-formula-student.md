**Role:** Electric Powertrain Engineer · **Year:** 2023 · **Domain:** EV traction, high-voltage subsystems, simulation

## Overview

Electric powertrain work for a **Formula Student** electric vehicle as part of **N1 Racing — the first team from Central Asia to officially participate in Formula Student.**

## What I did

- Worked on **traction and high-voltage subsystem** calculations, simulations, and component selection.
- Contributed to the design of a **competition-ready electric race car**.
- Helped represent the first Central Asian team to officially compete in Formula Student.
