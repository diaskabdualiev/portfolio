**Role:** Co-Founder / Robotics Engineer, Alashed LLC · **Years:** 2025 – 2026 · **Stack:** Raspberry Pi 4/5 & Pico, C/C++, Python, motion control, 3D printing

## Overview

As **co-founder of Alashed LLC**, I build **educational robotics kits** — a quadruped and a manipulator — for schools, owning the full cycle from hardware concept to control logic, fabrication, and documentation.

## What I built

- Developed a **quadruped robot kit** covering mechanical design, actuator/component selection, and the fundamentals of locomotion and control.
- Designed a **robotic manipulator kit** for hands-on learning in embedded systems, C/C++ and Python programming, and motion control.
- Built prototypes on **Raspberry Pi 4/5 and Pico**, and produced **assembly instructions** for classroom and demonstration use.
- Own the end-to-end pipeline: hardware concept → control logic → fabrication → documentation.
