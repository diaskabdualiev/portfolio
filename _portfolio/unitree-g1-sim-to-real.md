## Overview

Practical work across a complete **Unitree G1 robotics stack** — connecting simulation, reinforcement-learning workflows, teleoperation, and real-robot-compatible deployment experiments.

## What I built / worked on

- Operated **Isaac Lab, MuJoCo, Unitree SDK2** and **DDS-based communication** for simulation and real-robot-compatible workflows.
- Explored **locomotion and mimic-training** pipelines for the Unitree G1, including simulation-based testing and policy execution.
- Set up **teleoperation and manipulation simulation** tooling for experiment configuration, replay, and robotics data preparation.
- Focused on the **practical integration** of environments, tooling, and robotics software components toward real-world experimentation.
